# Code of Conduct

Differend Labs exists so that people without a technical background can understand AI. That only works if everyone feels welcome to ask, disagree, and point out mistakes.

## what we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the topic.
- Assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No question is too basic. Answer it plainly or leave it for someone who can.
- When you are wrong, say so. Publishing failures is part of how this lab works, and that applies to conversations too.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries.

## what is not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discrimination or slurs of any kind, including about background, gender, race, disability, religion, or experience level
- Mocking someone for not knowing a term
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Sexual content or unwanted attention
- Repeatedly pushing a conversation after being asked to stop

## where this applies

All Differend Labs spaces: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and lab social accounts. It also applies when someone represents the lab elsewhere.

## reporting

Email [hello@differend.ai](mailto:hello@differend.ai) with "Conduct" in the subject. Reports are kept private. You will get a reply, and the outcome can include a warning, removal of content, or a ban from lab spaces.
